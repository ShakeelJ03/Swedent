Frequently asked questions

How Swedent works, where listings come from and how your data is handled.

What is Swedent?

Swedent is a job and research platform for students at universities in Stockholm. It brings together research assistant positions, student jobs, thesis projects, PhD positions and research group profiles from official sources.

Who can use Swedent?

Swedent is free for all students and recent graduates, regardless of programme or field. Listings cover engineering, medicine, natural sciences, social sciences, economics and the humanities.

Where do the listings come from?

Job listings are taken from the official job boards of KTH, Karolinska Institutet, Stockholm University and the Stockholm School of Economics. Thesis projects come from the posting pages of companies, research institutes and research groups. Lab profiles are compiled from research group pages, staff profiles, publications, grants and past thesis lists. Every listing links to its original source.

How do I find a thesis project?

Open Thesis projects to see current projects with their deadlines, research groups that take thesis students, and the universities' own thesis portals. Most projects for the spring semester are posted in October and November.

How often are listings updated?

Job listings are checked every morning. New positions are added after they are verified against the official posting, and positions are removed once the application deadline has passed. Lab profiles are reviewed weekly.

How do I apply for a position?

Applications are submitted directly to the employer through the official posting. Swedent does not process applications or share your details with employers.

How does CV Match work?

CV Match reads your CV, works out your field and level, and compares them with every lab, open position and thesis project. Results from other fields are left out, even if they use the same tools. Each result lists the specific points that match and links to the source. Your CV is only processed when you start a match and is not stored unless you choose to save it.

How is my personal data handled?

Your profile, saved items and alerts are visible only to you. They are stored in your account when you are signed in, or on your device otherwise. You can delete all of your data at any time from your account page.

Which universities are included?

KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Karolinska Institutet, Stockholm University and the Stockholm School of Economics are included today. Södertörn University, GIH, Konstfack, the Royal College of Music, Stockholm University of the Arts, Sophiahemmet University, the Swedish Red Cross University, the Swedish Defence University and Ersta Sköndal Bräcke University College are being added.

Is Swedent affiliated with the universities?

No. Swedent is an independent platform. University names are used only to identify where a position or research group is based.
